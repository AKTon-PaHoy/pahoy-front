## What is this project?

- We are building a new platform to **connect local talent with clients who need services and products** delivered in person or physically.
- The goal is to **make it easy to find these talents** through a catalog of skills or services **offered by individuals**.
    - These services are typically **short in duration and informal in nature**.
- We use **geographic proximity** between service providers and clients as the starting point.
- Through this we aim to reconnect fragmented communities with the talent that already exists within them, reforming and strengthening their social fabric.
- We also want to **support individuals in vulnerable situations**, such as older adults, by **prioritizing their inclusion in their community's economic activity**.

## Who is it for?

**Entrepreneurs and independent workers** — especially individuals without formal employment but with **talents and skills they can monetize** — as well as potential clients who don't know them and could benefit from their work.

## What problem do we solve?

We **connect hidden talent** within communities **with local work demand**.

## Where do we see this project in 5–10 years?

We want to be an organization that offers a catalog of products, services, and tools aimed at entrepreneurs — always focused on recovering the value of work and reconnecting fragmented communities. We want to have the capacity to help those communities and address different causes of the social distress and decay they experience.

## What values define our personality?

Talent, Community, Work, Honesty, Commitment, Opportunity
