# Product Overview

## What is Pa·Hoy?

Pa·Hoy is a platform that connects local talent with clients who need services and products delivered in person or physically.

## Core Purpose

- **Connect hidden talent** within communities with local work demand
- Enable **entrepreneurs and independent workers** to monetize their skills and talents
- Prioritize **geographic proximity** as the starting point for matching services with clients

## Target Users

**Entrepreneurs and independent workers** — especially individuals without formal employment but with talents and skills they can monetize — as well as potential clients who don't know them and could benefit from their work.

## The Problem We Solve

We bridge the gap between hidden local talent and those who need their services. We reconnect fragmented communities by reforming and strengthening their social fabric through economic opportunity.

## Core Values

- **Talent** - Recognizing and celebrating individual skills
- **Community** - Strengthening local connections
- **Work** - Supporting economic opportunity
- **Honesty** - Transparent and authentic interactions
- **Commitment** - Dedicated to long-term community impact
- **Opportunity** - Enabling access and inclusion
